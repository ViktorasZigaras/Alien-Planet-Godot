# B08 survival and combat review

Godot 4.5.1 import and all eleven suites passed, including 19 B08 checks.
The B08 suite covers preview parity, garrison protection, expedition departure,
wounded withdrawal, emergency supply and save validation.

F5 opens the existing map with one rifle Soldier at Landing Station.
Choose a cohort in the bottom panel, click a hex and use March or Retreat.
Click a visible animal for the fire damage and duel odds previews.
Fire or Duel uses one movement point and stops the current route.
The preview and action share the combat formulas. Rifle range is two
hexes, flamer range is one with a stronger animal hit. Range uses hex
distance for this trial. Multiple visible animals in one hex are resolved
by the first animal in the displayed selection order. Interception and
zones of control remain open.

Train a Soldier through the existing training job. At the station, equipping
a rifle costs one unreserved Industry or a flamer costs two. The new equipment
takes effect immediately and the Soldier can move on the next turn.

Wildlife can pursue and attack in B08. Ready station Soldiers draw attacks
away from civilians sheltering on the station hex. Away from shelter, a
threatened civilian can retreat via the ordinary land route. Field workers
leave their job to evacuate and return to resident status on arrival.
Food and Energy shortages multiply combat strength with the wound factor.
Supply recovery restores the shortage factor, and wounds recover separately
after a quiet turn. Defeated cohorts are incapacitated at one HP and can
retreat. Work and attacks are unavailable until recovery to 35 HP.

Auto defence orders civilian retreats, reserves one garrison and withdraws
field Soldiers below 45 HP. It looks for Food-safe emergency generation
when Energy runs out. The player authorizes an expedition against an observed
animal. The general holds if the station is threatened or the ready expedition
force lacks a 25 percent margin over the observed target group. Field Soldiers
advance to reachable staging hexes and use legal ranged actions. The plan
reassesses on later turns. This is a small single-settlement tactical trial.
Full campaign force balance and the integrated UI belong to subsequent review.
