# B10 local expansion trial

Deploy a civilian with the reusable kit in Colony. Move them to clear land at least three hexes from Landing Station and existing outposts. In the Frontier panel under Orders, found for 4 Food, 3 Minerals and 2 unreserved Industry. The founder remains the same cohort, staffs the outpost and releases the kit. Choose an adjacent Food hex and assign it. If Landing Station currently works the hex, the assignment transfers and the earlier worker goes idle.

A deployed builder can lay a road underfoot for 1 Industry. Connect neighboring road hexes to use the existing one-third movement cost before river penalties. Roads remain after outpost loss. The Survey Compact is a fixed, passive faction contact with distinct ownership, shown after observation. Diplomacy is scheduled for B12.

A healthy Soldier within one hex protects a staffed outpost against nearby predators. An exposed outpost suspends work and is overrun after two exposed turns. Its crew survives and can refound after resupply. Evacuating an outpost releases its work hex. Landing Station cannot be voluntarily abandoned in this build.

This trial uses the existing Landing Station economy and a shared stockpile. Outposts have one founder and one Food assignment each, with no separate growth, local storage, construction queue or faction AI. The recovery check stages hostile pressure. The 1080p screenshot uses a played founding route from the opening stockpile with automatic defence off. Later work covers general settlement economies and recovery if the main base itself can be destroyed.
