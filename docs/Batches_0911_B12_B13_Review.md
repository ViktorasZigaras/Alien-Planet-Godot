# B12–B13 playtest

F5 opens B13 on seed 731. The B12 scene is available separately, with its own save slot. The B13 build includes the civilian, Explorer and rifle soldier visual pass.

## B12 · Survey Compact

The Civilization IV reference inventory includes lump-sum and recurring exchanges, Open Borders, maps and defensive pacts. This trial adapts lump-sum exchange and passage. The Compact is an independent counterparty whose finite inventories and current terms determine acceptance. It offers five turns of civilian entry for 2 unreserved Minerals, or 3 Food for 2 unreserved Minerals from its six-Food reserve. It accepts at most one ration exchange per turn. Soldiers cannot enter under the agreement. After expiry, civilians already there can leave. Route searches and step execution enforce the border.

## B13 · Physical services

The Independent Relay and the Compact Exchange each offer one service per player turn. A ready civilian must be on the tile. At the Compact, active passage is also required. The two choices are 2 Food from an eight-Food stock, or 3 Science for 2 unreserved Minerals from a twelve-Science stock. Merchant training uses the ordinary paid two-turn course. A Merchant receives 5 Science on the same paid exchange. The alternative doubling rule would yield 6. This trial uses only the +2 rule to conserve scarce site stock. Both outputs enter the normal economy. Research still requires its usual operational evidence.

The test uses one playable faction and a rule-driven Compact. NPC travel, competition for simultaneous visitor slots, recurrent contracts, information exchange, restocking and negotiated counteroffers are outside this trial. Starting-position balance still needs playtesting.

## Evaluation route

1. Select the Explorer on seed 731 and head toward hex 6,6. Inspect the Independent Relay and the neighboring Survey Compact. Try ordering entry into the Compact with the border closed.
2. In Orders, buy civilian passage and move the Explorer to the Compact. Check whether its services make the 2 Minerals feel worth paying. Compare with visiting the neutral relay directly.
3. At the relay, use either Food or Science, then try the other service during the same turn. Check what the stock strip, site inventory and refusal message show.
4. On a fresh opening, train a Merchant from Colony, deploy them with the kit, and compare the benefit against two turns of training and travel. Try another seed if time allows.
