# B06 exploration review

Godot 4.5.1 import and all nine suites passed. B06 passed 15 checks covering
travel, identification, exclusive work, reward accounting, saved knowledge,
hidden changes and scene launch. Results are stored under reports/b06.

F5 opens the same single-settlement map with one additional Explorer. The
overview exposes coastline, basic terrain, rivers and relief. Local visibility
covers the settlement work area and one hex around each field unit. Darker
tiles distinguish unobserved areas from remembered areas. Select a hex to see
its knowledge status. A question mark is an observed unidentified signal.

Right-click a destination to move the Explorer and use End turn for longer
routes. Try the signal at 1,3. Identify on the site to name the bonus and make
ordinary identified bonuses available to settlement workers. Wait for a
stationary turn, then Investigate to collect two Science once at turn end.
Alien Specimens provisionally require Power Systems and investigation before
use. Fieldwork then earns the special yield and one Science and one Culture
each turn. Fieldwork occupies the site exclusively and travel clears it.
Use Stop fieldwork to release the site. The Explorer eats one Food per turn.

Route planning uses the available observations for forest and road costs,
avoids currently observed hostiles, and uses basic geography elsewhere.
Movement resolves actual terrain costs and stops at a blocked step. The
settlement planner respects identification and exclusive jobs. The exploration
panel advises on investigation eligibility and projected Food shortages.
The player orders exploration and decisions to wait. This fixture has dormant
fauna, so live wildlife encounters belong to a later batch.

B06 has a separate save. Stored observations, identification and investigation
history persist across save/load. Hidden live developments are replaced with
their last observation in the map projection. Existing B01–B05 scenes remain
available. Next B07 covers foundational ecology, obstructions and settlement
pressure.
