# Population sprite generation

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, with transparent background enabled. The PNG is used unchanged; atlas rectangles are defined separately for the eight roles.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production sprite atlas for a small retro science-fiction planetary settlement game. Create a single 4-column by 2-row sprite sheet on a genuinely transparent alpha background, preferably 2048 by 1024 pixels. Eight separate FULL BODY human population-role sprites, one centered in each equal-sized cell, no overlap, large generous transparent gutters, identical scale and foot baseline within each cell. Pixel-art / crisp 1990s isometric strategy-game aesthetic, slightly chunky readable proportions, three-quarter front view facing slightly right, coherent shared body proportions and lighting. ALL headwear, feet and carried tools fully visible. Row 1 left to right: (1) Worker, brown-orange work clothes and small shovel; (2) Farmer, olive-green practical clothes, straw hat, seed bag and small hoe; (3) Engineer, yellow hard hat, blue-grey coveralls and tool case; (4) Merchant, burgundy coat, satchel and small handheld account ledger. Row 2 left to right: (5) Scientist, white laboratory coat over blue clothing, handheld sample vial and scanner; (6) Administrator, dark navy formal expedition uniform, clipboard, restrained gold trim; (7) Security, blue-grey protective expedition armour and a holstered sidearm, calm guard pose; (8) General technical Specialist, teal-grey technician uniform with handheld diagnostic tablet. Mixed adult faces and genders are fine; consistent small readable sprites. No text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no panels, no scenery, NO FLOOR TILES, NO ground patches, NO cast shadows outside the character, NO background, no white backdrop, no painted checkerboard. Each character is an isolated cutout. Keep a utilitarian grounded futuristic expedition mood, earthy and muted colours with clear profession differences.
